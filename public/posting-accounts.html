Post to The Highlander app

The Highlander app brings local news and community updates to people across the Highlands. Reading is free and doesn't need an account.

Posting accounts are free There are no fees, subscriptions or payments of any kind.

Who can get a posting account

Local organizations and groups that serve Haliburton County, such as:

  • Municipalities and townships
  • Health services
  • Schools and education programs
  • Non-profits and community groups
  • The Highlander's own reporters

The Highlander reviews every request before an account is created.

How to apply

Email us with:

  • Your organization's name
  • A contact person and phone number
  • A sentence about what you'd like to post
Email publisher@thehighlander.ca

Or write to publisher@thehighlander.ca from any email app.

What happens next

  1. We review your request.
  2. If it's approved, we create your account and email you the login details.
  3. In the app, tap Business login, then Log In, and start posting.

Posting guidelines

  • Share local, accurate information.
  • No spam, hate, harassment or illegal content.
  • The Highlander may remove posts or accounts that break these rules.
  • Readers can report any post from inside the app, and our editors review every report.
